## Bonus: free registration platforms (not cold email, sign up + get discovered)

Different channel from the above — you create a profile once and casting directors browse
it, rather than you emailing them directly. All say free registration; double-check that
still holds and there's no fee before you upload anything:

- **Talentrack** — https://www.talentrack.in — large India-wide casting call platform, actors/models profiles, casting directors post calls.
- **ACMODASI** — https://www.acmodasi.in — India-focused actor/model/dancer/singer database, no registration fee per their site.
- **CastYou** — https://castyou.in — India digital casting agency network across Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, Delhi, Bangalore.
- **Castkro** — https://www.castkro.com — free platform for acting/modelling auditions and casting calls across India.
